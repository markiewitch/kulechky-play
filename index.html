Kulechky

A small strategy game of marbles and planets

Scroll down to play ↓
  1. Each planet makes marbles for its owner.
  2. Send marbles along the links to take neutral and enemy planets.
  3. A level takes from 30 seconds to a few minutes.
  4. Play the campaign, or one new map for every day of the year.
